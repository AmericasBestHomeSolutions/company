Americas Best Home Solutions LLC

We buy houses, and we sell them without a bank.

A Wyoming limited liability company operating in ten states. We acquire residential property from owners and listing agents, and we sell it directly to buyers under written owner-financing agreements.

What the company does

We buy houses, then sell them to the people who want to live in them — without sending anyone to a bank.

We buy

Single-family houses from owners, listing agents, estates and landlords who want a straightforward, certain sale.

We sell

Those homes directly to buyers, carrying the balance ourselves under a written owner-financing agreement.

We are a real estate company — not a bank, a mortgage lender, or a licensed mortgage broker, and we do not offer loans or credit of any kind. The buyer and seller agree on a price, a down payment, a monthly amount and a term, all set out in a written contract before anything changes hands.

How we operate

  • Everything in writing. Price, down payment, monthly amount and term are set out in the agreement before anyone signs, and buyers are free to have it reviewed by anyone they choose.
  • Nobody buys sight unseen. Every home is available to walk through in person before a commitment is made.
  • Real people on the phone. Calls and texts to (551) 550-9906 reach our team directly.
  • Straight answers. We take a limited number of buyers at a time, and we would rather tell someone quickly that a home is not a fit than keep them waiting.

Get in touch

Buyers looking for a home should start at getownerfinancedhomes.com or casasconfinanciamiento.com, where every available property is listed. Owners and agents with a property to sell, and anyone with a question about the company itself, can reach us on the contact page.
