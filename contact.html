About text messages from us

We send text messages only to people who have given us express consent to do so — by checking the optional consent box on one of our listing sites, by telling us verbally on a call, or by replying to a written request. Consent is never a condition of buying a home, selling us a property, or getting a reply to any question.

You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP or contacting us above. The full terms are on our Messaging Terms page, and how we handle your information is set out in our Privacy Policy.
